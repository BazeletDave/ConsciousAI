# Institutional Power Amplification and the AI Paradox

Grand Research Institute | Independent public research brief | 19 October 2026

**Problem:** AI can accelerate institutional decisions faster than oversight and correction mechanisms adapt.

**GRI research proposition:** Examine access, capability, authority, consequences and feedback as interdependent dimensions of institutional power. Historical IMF, WTO, IFC, Dell, Intel, 1MDB and Cambodian antiquities cases offer comparative institutional mechanisms, not historical AI deployments.

**Method:** Collect dated public primary evidence, separate verified facts from interpretations and counterfactuals, code decision authority and outcomes, test rival explanations and preserve revisions.

**Candidate measures:** Governance latency, appeal effectiveness, traceability of delegated authority, access barriers, institutional durability and distributional outcomes. These are proposed metrics, not validated scores.

**Relevant public standards:** NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, Canadian AIA, OECD, UNESCO and Council of Europe. See the [comparative crosswalk](../Integrated-Research-Journey/2026-10-19-global-government-ai-governance-evidence-and-instruments.md).
