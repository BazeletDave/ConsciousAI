# GRI Academic Integrity and Originality Review

Preliminary review | 19 October 2026

The GRI AI Paradox and Institutional Power Amplification Framework are presented as independent research propositions. Thunderbird teaching, faculty scholarship and historical cases are attributed inputs, not GRI discoveries. No faculty or university endorsement is implied.

**Open checks:** Verify specific course and competition rules before any reuse of graded submissions; review copyrighted cases, HBR reprints and personal materials; conduct a documented literature novelty search; audit factual and quantitative claims against primary records; verify ownership and license implications; distinguish human-authored intellectual contributions from AI-assisted drafting.

This document is a review protocol, not ASU clearance, a completed originality scan, legal advice or proof of compliance.

Policy starting points: https://provost.asu.edu/academic-integrity/policy and https://copyright.gov/ai/ .
