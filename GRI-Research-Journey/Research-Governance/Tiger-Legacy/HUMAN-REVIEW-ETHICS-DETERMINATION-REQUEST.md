# GRI Human Review | Ethics Determination Request Package

**Status:** Draft for submission to an appropriate qualified ethics review authority. Not submitted and no IRB exemption or approval has been granted.

## Project summary

Grand Research Institute proposes a prospective study of adult human reviewers making decisions about entirely synthetic AI governance cases. Research compares standard versus governance enriched evidence packages and examines unsafe approvals, unnecessary denials, appropriate escalations, time to decision and documented rationale. There are no real client records, operational actions, or live security tests.

## Determination requested

Request written guidance on whether this activity constitutes human subjects research under applicable law and institutional policy; whether an IRB, independent ethics committee or other review is required; and whether any exemption or expedited review is appropriate. Independent GRI research does not inherit Thunderbird or ASU ethics oversight.

## Materials required before submission

- Full protocol and final independently adjudicated case bank
- Recruitment population, inclusion and exclusion criteria, compensation and conflicts
- Participant information and consent language including withdrawal limitations
- Data dictionary, pseudonymization, access controls, retention and deletion schedule
- Risk and benefit assessment, debriefing, contact and complaint pathway
- Sample size justification and preregistered statistical analysis
- Named responsible investigator, jurisdiction and institutional ethics contact

## Risk and confidentiality

Potential risks include inconvenience, perceived evaluation pressure and disclosure of participant responses. Mitigate through voluntary participation, minimized data collection, no real consequences, restricted access and aggregate publication. Do not collect unnecessary identifiers or use actual confidential institutional records.
