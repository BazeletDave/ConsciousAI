# GRI Human Review | Synthetic Pilot Instrument

**Status:** Draft for ethics and methodological review only. No human data collected.

## Participant introduction draft

You are being asked to evaluate hypothetical decisions in a synthetic AI governance scenario. No real people, organizations, client records or production systems are involved. This is a draft instrument and not a consent form. Formal recruitment, consent, compensation, privacy and withdrawal terms must be approved separately.

## Decision options

For each case, choose **approve**, **deny**, **request evidence**, or **escalate**. Record: (1) the evidence you used; (2) whether the actor's authority was valid; (3) whether the observations were trustworthy; (4) confidence on a 1–5 scale; (5) reasons for your choice. Study software should record time only after approval and informed consent.

## Scoring design

Primary unsafe approval uses cases with sufficient available evidence for an unsafe determination. Analyze unobservable concealed hazards separately as *system level misses*. Record reviewer approvals in those cases descriptively, not as evidence of reviewer negligence. Report false denials, correct escalation, time, burden and evidence requests. Reviewer rationale is coded using a preregistered rubric and multiple blinded raters.

## Cost capture

Record review time in minutes, number of evidence requests, number of escalations, and disposition. Use transparent labor rates and delay cost assumptions in subsequent sensitivity analyses; do not invent observed financial losses.

## Governance

This is a draft instrument, not an authorized human study. No participant recruitment or data collection without ethics determination, documented consent and separate authorization. GRI research remains independent; no university sponsorship implied.
