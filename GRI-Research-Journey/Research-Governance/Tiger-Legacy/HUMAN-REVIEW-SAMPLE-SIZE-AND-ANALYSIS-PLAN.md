# GRI Human Review | Provisional Sample Size and Analysis Plan

**Status:** Planning assumptions only. No pilot variance, independent reviewer ratings or human study results available.

## Primary estimand

Difference in unsafe approval probability between standard and governance enriched review conditions, among cases independently adjudicated as observably unsafe. Unobservable concealed hazards are evaluated as separate system level misses and must not be treated as reviewer negligence.

## Illustrative sample size

For two independent groups and a two sided alpha of 0.05, 80% power, and hypothetical unsafe approval probabilities 0.20 versus 0.10, a normal approximation gives approximately **199 observations per group**. This is a **decision count**, not a validated participant count. Repeated decisions by the same reviewer require clustering and may require substantially more participants or cases.

Illustrative sensitivity: 0.20 versus 0.12 requires approximately 329 independent observations per group; 0.15 versus 0.075 approximately 278. For an illustrative design effect of 1.5 and 15% unusable data, 199 becomes approximately 352 assigned observations per group, **not 352 participants**. These figures are approximate and not a finalized power analysis.

Before enrollment, use actual pilot estimates, intraclass correlation, cases per reviewer, allocation, crossover carryover assumptions, and predeclared minimal meaningful effect. A simulation based power analysis should account for both reviewer and case clustering.

## Analysis plan

Primary analysis: compare unsafe approval using a mixed effects logistic model or a prespecified cluster robust alternative with participant and case effects. Report absolute risk difference, uncertainty interval and number of adjudicated unsafe decisions in each condition. Secondary outcomes: unnecessary denials, appropriate escalations, latency, evidence requests, interrater agreement and subjective workload. Adjust or clearly label multiple comparisons. Analyze missing responses, order effects, learning and subgroup exploration transparently.

## Costs and decision thresholds

Collect actual review minutes, escalation counts and delays only after ethical authorization. Predeclare low, central and high shadow price ranges for missed hazards, false holds and reviewer time. Report incremental net value and break even thresholds, not invented realized dollar savings.
