# GRI Tiger Legacy | Mission and Integration Guardrails

**Grand Research Institute | David A. Grand**  
**Status:** Binding development criteria for the independent research prototype; not evidence of completed integration.

## Mission alignment

Tiger Legacy extends GRI's AI Paradox and institutional governance research into cybersecurity assurance. Its central research question is whether apparently compliant local controls produce verifiable, accountable end-to-end outcomes. Preserve human authority, revocation, contestability, provenance, independent evaluation, and remediation accountability.

## Training a Tiger continuity

- Treat the original Training a Tiger experiment, protocol, frozen results, and documented failure boundaries as independent historical research artifacts.
- Reuse the evaluation *method*—hidden shifts, independent observation, ablations, negative outcomes, and end-to-end testing—where appropriate.
- Do not repurpose Training a Tiger experimental performance percentages as cybersecurity performance metrics.
- Link new Tiger Legacy findings back to Training a Tiger with explicit source, methodology, test scope, and limitations. Never rewrite prior results to fit a new hypothesis.

## Existing GRI dashboard constraints

- Existing dashboard remains the research reporting interface; Tiger Legacy is a distinct synthetic-only assurance data producer, not an autonomous control plane.
- No autonomous operational control, real-world remediation, scanning, external security actions, client data, credentials, confidential audit findings, or personally identifying records.
- Dashboard reporting must be read-only, aggregate, provenance-linked, and clearly labeled **synthetic research** or **unmeasured** as applicable.
- Do not introduce new dashboard routes, data formats, scheduled jobs, or deployments before inspecting current implementation contracts, authentication, operational limits, and security boundaries.
- Preserve existing collection cadence, resource budgets, data integrity, and unrelated GRI research features; no assumption of compatibility before integration tests.
- A dashboard visualization does not constitute security enforcement or independent certification.

## Engineering release controls

1. Inspect the actual dashboard code and existing Training a Tiger/Tiger Assurance interfaces.
2. Record explicit compatibility mapping and a threat model; flag unknowns.
3. Run and retain reproducible unit, adversarial, integration, and regression results.
4. Propose the smallest read-only synthetic adapter with no production authority.
5. Verify no regression to existing dashboard collection and governance reporting.
6. Update engineering files, research methods, negative findings, and status evidence together.
7. Require explicit review before merging to main or deploying to Render.
