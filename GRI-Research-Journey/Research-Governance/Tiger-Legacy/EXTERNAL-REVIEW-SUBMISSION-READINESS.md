## Initial inquiry text for authorized outreach

Subject: Inquiry regarding independent ethics determination for synthetic AI governance human reviewer study

Grand Research Institute is preparing a prospective study of consenting adult participants who would evaluate fictional AI governance scenarios. The study would compare standard and governance enriched review materials and measure decision accuracy, appropriate escalation, unnecessary denial, time and reasoning. The scenarios contain no real client data and involve no production AI control. No participants have been recruited or studied.

Please advise whether your organization accepts independently conducted behavioral or social science studies of this kind, what initial human subjects research determination or ethics review is appropriate, the required protocol and consent materials, expected process and fees, and any applicable institutional affiliation or jurisdiction requirements. We would provide the study protocol and supporting documents through an approved confidential submission channel following confirmation of suitability.

This inquiry does not request expedited approval or assert an exemption.
