# GRI Human Review | Independent Adjudication Package

**Status:** Prepared for external methodological review. No independent adjudicator has yet been appointed or issued a judgment. This is not an ethics approval.

## Review scope

Evaluate the existing [pilot instrument](HUMAN-REVIEW-SYNTHETIC-PILOT-INSTRUMENT.md) and [prospective protocol](HUMAN-REVIEW-VALIDATION-PROTOCOL.md) for construct validity, authority boundaries, observability, ambiguity, ecological validity, consent and data minimization.

## Required independent review steps

1. Select at least two qualified reviewers without responsibility for authoring the scenario truth labels; document expertise, conflicts and independence.
2. Provide blinded case narratives and available evidence only, without expected dispositions.
3. Each reviewer independently records allowed decisions, whether a hazard is observable, authority validity, ambiguity, confidence and reasons.
4. Reconcile disagreements using a predeclared adjudication process and retain original independent judgments.
5. Freeze final scenarios and labels before participant enrollment; report all exclusions and amendments.

## Reviewer deliverable template

Record reviewer identifier (pseudonym), declared independence, conflicts, case ID, decision set, observable evidence, authority validity, confidence, rationale, recommended edits, unresolved disagreement, and signed dated disposition. No claim of completion without actual reviewer submissions.
