## November 18, 2026 | Confidential external review and disclosure authority

Grand Research Institute may engage in external scholarly, technical and institutional review, consultation, and quality assurance. Such engagement must be distinguished from completed independent evaluations, formal certifications, institutional approvals, endorsements, or adoption. No such status is presumed from correspondence, feedback, or affiliation.

**Default disclosure classification:** Reviewer identities, affiliations linked to specific reviews, correspondence, assessments, recommendations, draft findings, unpublished evidence and deliberations remain restricted unless an authorized GRI decision maker confirms disclosure authority and applicable reviewer, employer, institutional, contractual and intellectual property permissions. Reported positive feedback is not itself permission to publish. Do not infer that the Board of Trustees has approved disclosure or assert that it has prohibited disclosure without an authorized record.

**Public statement:** “Grand Research Institute maintains procedures for external scholarly, technical and institutional review of its research and associated systems. Review materials and findings are subject to applicable confidentiality, intellectual property and disclosure requirements. The existence of review or consultation does not imply endorsement, certification or approval by any individual or affiliated institution. Public release of review findings requires appropriate authorization.”

**Review evidence controls:** Keep source assessments, access permissions, conflicts, reviewer consent, decision records and any release approvals in a restricted review register rather than the public repository. A public statement of review completion, independence or outcome requires verifiable scope, dated documentation and release authority; otherwise describe only the established procedure. Publish redacted summaries only when authorized, without reidentification risk. Preserve historical records and prior rights; this policy is not a retroactive license amendment or claim of external certification.
