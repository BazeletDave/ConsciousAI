## Implications for the GRI experiment

Define a bounded putting task before selecting a robot or simulator. Compare an original GRI goal proximal curriculum with a forward curriculum and a fixed difficulty baseline under matched compute, training steps and seeds. Freeze the holdout before tuning progression gates. Keep nominal trials, new surface conditions, changed target distances and mechanical perturbations as separately reported evaluation sets.

Record the initial state check, target estimate, policy version, stage, allowed action, trial outcome, residual distance, reset or escalation, training budget and evidence reference for every trial. Report first attempt performance alongside cumulative success. A long success streak can coexist with poor recovery or weak transfer.

The mastery threshold is an experimental design choice to be specified in advance, not a numerical ML requirement attributed to Earl Woods. Keep a holdout test outside the progression rule. Compare failure recovery and performance after difficulty changes, with uncertainty across independent runs.

The institutional comparison concerns delegated scope and the ability to obtain correction. In executive education, assess whether a leader recognizes the boundary of personal or model competence, obtains appropriate specialist help and retains accountable ownership. Use the existing decision episode instrument for authority, intervention and remediation records. Additional confidence or learning measures require separate validation.

## A refined AI Paradox

A system may improve its scored success while becoming worse at detecting uncertainty, recovering from a miss or requesting help. The research must therefore compare task success with the institution's ability to challenge, pause, correct and escalate. Earl's recognition of specialist expertise supplies a substantive source connection to this question.

Competitive pressure is not interchangeable with adversarial noise. The supplied page 70 supports the former. Visual distraction, sensor occlusion, wind perturbations and mechanical jitter remain independent experimental mechanisms until specifically supported and evaluated. No result in these photographs proves a robotics transfer effect.
