## Refining the research hypothesis

The supported proposition is that an instructional progression from simpler actions toward broader skill can inspire a controlled curriculum experiment. Formal RL backward chaining, goal proximal initialization, reward design, perturbation schedules and mastery gates are GRI engineering choices that require explicit definitions and matched evaluation.

The foreword broadens the leadership comparison. The research should examine support, initiative, enjoyment, disagreement and values alongside performance and robustness. Human learners and automated policies have different forms of agency. A robot's reward signal cannot establish that it has human understanding, motivation or moral judgment.

For the AI Paradox, the testable question becomes whether improved task results coexist with diminished ability to challenge a decision, obtain help or correct an error. The existing decision episode instrument can record authority, trace completeness, interventions and reviewed remediation. Additional human learning measures need their own sampling and assessment protocol.
