## Academic interpretation

Roy C. Nelson's institutional political economy teaching provides **three competing lenses**, liberalism, economic nationalism and structuralism. GRI independently applies these to questions of institutional authority, market incentives, bargaining power and legitimacy. It is not a factual source for Uber pricing, cybersecurity incidents or IMF event occurrence. [Nelson methodological boundaries](../../Integrated-Research-Journey/nelson-model-methodological-boundaries.md).

For algorithmic organizational control, the GRI Uber engineering study also identifies Kellogg, Valentine and Christin (2020), *Academy of Management Annals*, DOI [10.5465/annals.2018.0174](https://doi.org/10.5465/annals.2018.0174). That framework informs interpretation; it does not verify individual company metrics. GLOBE is relevant only when the research question concerns intercultural leadership, and does not explain individual conduct based on names or presumed nationality.
