# GRI Continuous Research Intake and Advancement Protocol

**Owner:** Grand Research Institute, founded by David Grand. **Status:** Independent research operations. No institutional endorsement is implied.

## Research intake for future graduate courses and professional work
Each new contribution starts as a research proposal or reference, not a verified observation. Register: intake ID; submission date; original author; source bibliographic citation and URL; whether drawn from a graduate course, public scholarship, professional practice or GRI fieldwork; case family; research question; proposed governance relevance; methodology; applicable permissions; privacy/consent restrictions; limitations; and requested next action.

**Never upload restricted course packets, Harvard Business School teaching-case content, graded submissions, exam materials, faculty teaching notes, or identifiable interviews to public repositories.** Link to lawfully accessible published sources, and write original analytical summaries. Distinguish GRI's founder's academic journey from institutional sponsorship.

## Lifecycle
1. **Proposed:** citation and rights status documented; no research claim promoted.
2. **Screened:** human steward checks rights, consent, conflicts, source reliability, and academic-integrity boundaries.
3. **Research candidate:** original GRI analysis and bounded claims with provenance; not independently verified.
4. **Under independent review:** reviewers and adjudicator are assigned and authenticated outside the public repository.
5. **Adjudicated:** disagreements resolved, digest frozen, limitations and rights documented.
6. **Benchmark eligible:** independently labeled, leakage-audited holdout frozen, class distribution and evaluation plan documented.
7. **Evaluated:** reproducible metrics compared with baselines, confidence intervals and errors documented.
8. **Release approved:** human authorization plus privacy, security, licensing, attribution and ethics checks.
9. **Monitored:** drift, source-health, correction requests, dataset revisions, and rollback readiness reviewed.

No automated transition past **Research candidate** without explicit authenticated human review.

## Advancement tracks
- **Institutional governance:** political economy, comparative institutions, regulatory authority, public accountability.
- **Leadership and negotiation:** practitioner-oriented communication, trust, cultural context, leadership decisions.
- **Algorithmic management:** dispatch, incentives, worker impact, platform operational controls.
- **Agentic governance:** delegated authority, tool permissions, oversight, exception handling, auditability.
- **AI assurance:** evidence quality, model robustness, provenance, monitoring, incident learning.

## Operational policy
Preserve the live dashboard and three-hour public-source collection. Quality checks should run on every relevant code change. Do not automatically train or promote models, ingest proprietary educational material, publish identifiable research subjects, or modify unrelated Render services. Source outages and stale data should be visible as degraded conditions, never silently treated as verified freshness.

## Evaluation maturity
The current nine-record metadata dataset with two coarse case families is **not** sufficient for a credible ML generalization study. Do not report a numerical model-performance comparison until independent reviewers, rights clearance, adequate cases, grouped holdout design and a reproducible baseline evaluation exist.

## Research status statement
GRI is an independent evolving research and governance intelligence platform. Implementation, scientific validation, operational resilience, and external benchmarking are distinct claims. Comparisons to government, university or frontier-lab platforms require a defined benchmark and independent evidence.
