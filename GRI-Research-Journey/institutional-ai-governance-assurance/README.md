# GRI Institutional AI Governance & Assurance

**Grand Research Institute | Independent research program**

This portfolio organizes applied research on AI governance, institutional accountability, cybersecurity assurance, compliance verification, and delegated agent authority. It is independent GRI research, not an official U.S. Treasury, university, or government program.

## Research streams

1. **Treasury AI Label Verification:** An exploratory labeling and decision support case study examining rule traceability, confidence, human adjudication, and audit evidence. Treasury references describe the case context, not government authorization or endorsement.
2. **Agentic Governance & Controls:** Delegated authority, capability boundaries, approvals, logging, and contestability.
3. **Cybersecurity & Runtime Assurance:** Runtime patch verification, provenance, operational recovery, and evidence integrity.
4. **Institutional Risk & Compliance:** Comparative policy, incentives, stakeholder impacts, and accountability frameworks.

## Evidence and publication standards

Distinguish research prototypes, hypothetical examples, source-supported institutional claims, and empirically validated outcomes. No operational deployment, regulatory approval, or independent certification is implied. Preserve existing Treasury experiment source material and historic paths as case evidence rather than rewriting their provenance.
