### Research principle

**Governance is the dependent question.** License counts, sales, land ownership, corporate transactions and financing are contextual variables. Each empirical claim must be connected to a documented governance mechanism and its competing explanations. A valid license or transaction does not by itself demonstrate good governance; an appeal or disagreement does not by itself demonstrate misconduct.
