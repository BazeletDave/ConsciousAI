# Living AI Ecosystem | Information Mobility, Algorithmic Authority and Institutional Adaptation

**Grand Research Institute | Research Journey**  
**Research lead:** David Grand  
**Research continuity:** 2021 to present  
**Collection version:** 1.0 | October 16, 2026  
**Status:** Exploratory research synthesis and conceptual case studies; not a validated empirical study.

## Purpose
This collection examines how information observed in physical or digital settings can be captured, transformed, combined, moved, and used by AI systems to inform or execute decisions. It asks how management and public institutions maintain authority, accountability and meaningful human oversight as these capabilities develop.

GRI does not propose a final institutional model. The work applies established scholarship and governance frameworks to emerging questions, distinguishing inherited theory from original interpretation.

## Interpretation and evidence policy
The smartphone example is a hypothetical, authorized exposure scenario; supplied images alone do not demonstrate actual covert collection or successful exfiltration. News screenshots are leads for source verification, not independent incident confirmation. Illustrative diagrams are not empirical measurements. Meeting screenshots document visible context, not endorsements. Third party frameworks require precise attribution, and organizational performance figures require primary-source verification. Do not publish private meeting captures or sensitive operational material without authorization.
