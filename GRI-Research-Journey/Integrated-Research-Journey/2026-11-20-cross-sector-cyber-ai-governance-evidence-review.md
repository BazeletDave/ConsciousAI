## Verified institutional baselines for service continuity

The FBI's NCIC privacy impact assessment describes two controls relevant to downstream reliance on criminal justice records. The agency that entered a record controls changes to it, and users confirm the validity and accuracy of an active record with the submitting agency before acting on it. These are documented operating rules. Their performance during disruption remains an empirical question. [FBI privacy impact assessment](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/pias/pia-ncic-020723.pdf/)

USDA's Farm Production and Conservation Business Center administers core financial functions including billing, payments and collections. Its public award payment instructions document how payment claims are submitted. These sources identify a service dependency and a baseline workflow. [Business Center functions](https://www.fpacbc.usda.gov/about) · [Award payment instructions](https://www.fpacbc.usda.gov/about/grants-and-agreements/award-payments)

Use these baselines in the existing assurance comparison to examine whether agency confirmation remains available before action on a record, and whether payment claims can be tracked, reconciled and processed after interruption. Incident attribution, affected records, service impact and measured recovery each require their own evidence.

## Institutional synthesis

**Hypothesis A — assurance lag:** Changes to complex and dependent systems may propagate operational risk faster than institutions can independently test, explain, contest or reverse those changes. Assess change tickets, staged rollouts, rollback evidence, recovery times and independent reproduction.

**Hypothesis B — agentic speed asymmetry:** Documented offensive multi-agent automation compresses attack timelines; assess whether defensive controls, authority and audit trails can keep pace. This is a testable asymmetry, not evidence that AI caused the NSA or banking outages.

**Hypothesis C — external control and concentration:** Contractual cloud capacity, financing and platform dependencies may concentrate operational authority across multiple firms. Measure concentration with properly separated contract obligations, equity, voting control and substitutability; do not label a cartel absent appropriate competition evidence.

**Hypothesis D — algorithmic identity governance:** LAX license/profile discrepancies offer a bounded observational comparator for GRI's existing Uber/Lyft work. Investigate verification frequency, appeal and correction pathways, identity substitution risks, enforcement false positives and division of responsibility. Do not use identifiable drivers or riders in the research ledger.
