## Intellectual trajectory

The founder's more than two decades of company leadership and management experience supply practical institutional observations, managerial decision contexts and research questions. Approximately three to four years of Thunderbird School of Global Management study provide the subsequent academic discipline through which these experiences are examined: comparative political economy, leadership and organizational behavior, strategy, finance, global development, analytical methods and emerging technology governance.

**Distinction:** Professional experience is not itself academic validation. Classroom learning is not itself an empirical result. GRI's original synthesis, hypotheses, case selection, field observations and technical prototypes are independent research outputs that require source tracing, methodological criticism and, where appropriate, external reproduction.

1. Identify a claim as **faculty-published scholarship**, **classroom-derived concept**, **documented external evidence**, **founder field observation**, or **independent GRI inference**.
2. Cite original publications, institutional biographies and course materials when available; do not present a faculty member's teaching as endorsement of GRI conclusions.
3. Attribute Roy C. Nelson's original institutional model accurately and label GRI's AI and cybersecurity extensions as original, testable interpretations.
4. Distinguish published research from academic credentials and course exposure; faculty reputation does not substitute for empirical verification.
