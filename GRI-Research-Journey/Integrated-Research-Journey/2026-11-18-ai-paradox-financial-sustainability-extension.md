## Research proposition

AI infrastructure investment may expand more quickly than the financial and institutional capacity to independently evaluate its returns, allocate its risks, and correct investment decisions. This is a **testable hypothesis**, not a finding of an AI financial crisis. The mechanism extends the existing AI Paradox question of whether optimization reshapes its own economic and institutional feedback environment.

## Institutional evaluation design

| Dimension | Proposed indicator | Evidence needed | Governance question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capital intensity | AI related capex / revenue and operating cash flow | Issuer filings and reconciled segment disclosures | Are investment assumptions reviewable? |
| Financing exposure | Debt issuance, maturity profile, interest burden, covenants | Bond prospectuses, debt notes, ratings methodologies | Who bears refinancing and downside risk? |
| Economic performance | Incremental revenue, utilization, margins and cash returns | Audited statements and independently testable attribution | Can returns be distinguished from speculative demand? |
| Infrastructure externalities | Grid load, generation, water and public incentives | Utility filings, regulatory records, public budgets | Are costs and benefits allocated transparently? |
| Institutional control | Review thresholds, scenario testing, independent assurance | Board and regulator documentation where public | Can decision makers contest or reverse commitments? |

**Rival explanations:** Normal investment cycles, accelerated depreciation, cash timing, changing business mix, cloud growth, and deliberate strategic investment may explain margin compression without demonstrating governance failure.

## Verification gates before publication as an empirical finding

1. Identify the original charts' publisher, date, model, sample boundaries and assumptions.
2. Reconcile each company margin against SEC filings and company investor relations statements, including fiscal quarter definitions and cash flow calculations.
3. Reconcile Treasury financing estimates with Treasury borrowing announcements and fiscal projections.
4. Separate announced capex, committed financing, actual debt issuance and realized cash expenditure.
5. Record source URL, retrieval date, calculation, revision history, uncertainty, and alternative explanations for each figure.
6. Only then evaluate cross sector exposure and any causal or systemic risk claims.
