## Fixed hypotheses
H1: Greater location-specific physical capital and infrastructure dependence are associated with higher prospective switching costs, conditional on other variables.
H2: A change in elected office does not itself constitute a regulatory or fiscal change; observed investment responses require evidence of the intervening policy mechanism.
H3: Site-selection optimization can understate institutional transition risk if governance and reversibility are excluded.
H4: Cross-jurisdictional diversification can alter exposure to localized institutional change; verify the actual legal entity and facility map before calculating concentration.

## Units and measures
Unit of analysis: `legal_entity × facility × jurisdiction × observation_date`, plus separate policy event records. Measure: capital expenditure (realized, period, scope), commitments (announced, not spent), gross asset balances (stock, not flow), grants (awarded vs disbursed), employment (planned vs observed), permits (applied/approved/denied), utility requirements, and decisions to build, expand, defer or cancel. Keep federal, state and municipal authority separate. Do not sum related-party sales, equity purchases, grants and project capital commitments as if they were equivalent.

## Comparison and causal limits
Descriptive event timeline is the default. Any difference-in-differences, interrupted time series or matched comparison must be separately approved and preregistered with valid comparator jurisdictions, pretrends, timing, parallel trends assessment, contemporaneous national shocks, sample limitations and negative controls. Candidate comparators include Tennessee compute assets and Tesla's non-Texas facilities only if economic comparability is established. No election-driven causal claim from simple before/after charts.

## Evidence classes
A: SEC filing, statute, executed contract, official certified result, regulatory order. B: issuer announcement, corporate release or documented policy proposal. C: independent reporting. D: hypothesis or synthetic model. Every data point retains URL, publisher, observation date, retrieval date, source passage, metric definition, units, geographic scope, status and review decision. Conflict resolution prioritizes original dated documents and preserves prior versions. Missing is not zero.
