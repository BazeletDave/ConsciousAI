# GRI Integrated Research and Implementation Dossier
## Leadership Learning Algorithmic Management and Institutional Assurance

David A. Grand | Grand Research Institute
28 October 2026 | Research implementation edition 1

GRI should expand from its existing research infrastructure through a shared evidence and evaluation program. The current foundation includes the public dashboard, executable assurance and mobility modules, ZERO’s dataset audit, Treasury originating prototype materials, deposited leadership history, and the Cambodian antiquities working case. Training a Tiger adds a documented learning sequence and a new experimental setting to this established program.

This dossier records work completed during the present review and specifies the next implementation decisions. The accompanying package contains source snapshots, source identity checks, executable lineage controls, tests, a claim register, a public endpoint snapshot, and reproducibility instructions. These are delivered research assets. They do not constitute a completed causal validation of the AI Paradox or a trained golf robot.

The organizing question is how institutions convert observations into representations, representations into decisions, and decisions into legitimate authority. The AI Paradox becomes concrete when measured task success diverges from the institution’s ability to understand, challenge, and correct the consequences.

## Executive decisions

Use ConsciousAI as the comparative research archive and dashboard integration location. Preserve ZERO as a distinct dataset provenance workstream. Reuse existing mobility and assurance components rather than building parallel systems with competing definitions. Treat Treasury materials as an independent design case. Preserve human management history as historical evidence with its own chronology. Extend the Cambodian case through primary institutional records and a structured event ledger.

Adopt the new offline evidence lineage layer as a development artifact for review. It detects duplicated sources, declared shared origins, unmeasured training claims, missing review, and requests beyond the research record. It does not issue factual certifications or operational permissions. The supplied dashboard review payload is a candidate integration artifact, not a new deployed API response.

The first scientific milestone should be a versioned decision episode register populated with reviewable evidence from the existing cases. Machine learning and curriculum experiments should use this evidence architecture, while retaining domain specific methods and outcomes. A unified research method does not require one universal model.

## A reproduced limitation and an implemented improvement

A synthetic counterexample supplied two different publisher names with the same source URL to the existing assurance gate. With the supplied review flags, the gate returned eligibility for human publication review. It continued to require human review and did not authorize remediation. The limitation is therefore narrow: publisher name diversity can be mistaken for independent evidence in this check. [R3]

The new lineage gate identifies the duplicate URL and shared declared origin and holds the same example for review. Twelve tests cover this issue and additional boundaries, including naive timestamps, missing sources, unmeasured training effects, contradictions, duplicate identifiers, and attempted external actions.

This is an implemented engineering improvement supported by a reproducible counterexample. It is not evidence of actual dashboard misinformation or a population estimate of error. Declared origin identifiers can themselves be wrong; human source review remains necessary. The old source is preserved unchanged so a reviewer can reproduce the comparison. [R3]

## Comparing human management and algorithmic management

Historical field mentoring offers a mechanism to investigate: supervisors observe performance, explain decisions, and require learners to teach others before advancing. Contemporary systems may translate observation into telemetry, explanation into interface guidance, and promotion criteria into scores. The similarities justify comparison of management mechanisms; they do not establish equivalent human experiences or identical causal effects.

The key dependent variables should include performance, retained diagnostic skill, discretion, opportunity, and correction. A faster task completion rate can coexist with a weaker ability to identify a bad instruction. The reverse is also possible: assistance may free time for learning. Both are empirical possibilities.

The executive education program should use documented GRI episodes. Participants can inspect a source chain, evaluate an automated recommendation, identify its authority, and propose a review or remedy. Their performance should be assessed on a fresh case after instruction and in a later organizational application. Attendance and satisfaction are educational measures, not demonstrated institutional improvement.

## Cambodian antiquities and institutional correction

The existing Legitimacy Paradox paper provides a framework of source integrity, provenance, independent validation, context, contestability, and remediation. This review strengthens that source base with primary records rather than treating its bibliography leads as completed verification. [R5]

The Met’s December 15, 2023 announcement initiated the return of fourteen sculptures to Cambodia and two to Thailand. Its July 2, 2024 announcement described physical return of the fourteen Cambodian sculptures. These are different institutional milestones, and their dates should be recorded separately. A 2023 decision date cannot substitute for a physical transfer date. [P1, P2]

DOJ records distinguish allegations against Douglas Latchford from subsequent legal outcomes. The indictment was dismissed after his death. A 2023 civil settlement is separate from a criminal conviction. The research must preserve those distinctions rather than use the prominence of the case to collapse different kinds of evidence. [P3]

The comparative AI question concerns how documents and prior institutional acceptance influence later decisions. It does not equate cultural heritage trafficking with ordinary AI errors. The positive comparison matters too: consultation, new evidence, institutional review, and return of objects illustrate correction and international cooperation. Cambodia, Thailand, the United States, and the museum have different roles and interests.

Build an object level ledger with accession identifier, asserted origin, source records, challenge, institutional response, legal event, decision, and transfer. Where an object’s historical acquisition or challenge date is unknown, leave it unknown. The time between two public announcements is not automatically the duration of wrongdoing, delay, or institutional awareness.

It should retain claim identifiers, distinguish source origin from publication domain, and keep contradictory evidence visible. Integration tests should verify that a stale or incomplete record does not silently become current or verified. Record event occurrence separately from collection time. Otherwise a recent dashboard refresh may appear to make old evidence new.

## Mobility Treasury ZERO and Waymax

GRI Mobility Intelligence already provides a deterministic read only offer evaluator. Its tests cover selected arithmetic and class, sleep, and return deadline constraints. The inspected public summary reports zero verified observations and zero trained models. Dataset 12 notes the need to distinguish accepted offers, completed trips, and settled payments, as well as counterfactual costs. These are concrete next data engineering requirements. [R6]

A prospective trip ledger should separate offered pay from settled pay, pickup time from trip time, waiting and return time, actual distance from estimates, and incurred cost from hypothetical cost. Collect declined offers where authorized to reduce selection bias. Participant observations do not reveal platform intent without additional evidence. The first analysis should describe the observed sample before attempting earnings forecasts or policy effects.

The Treasury originating materials supply a useful authority case: exact field matching, ambiguity, extraction error, and human adjudication. Their stakeholder narratives are supplied exercise material, not authenticated interviews. No federal production access or endorsed service is asserted. A local benchmark can compare clean and degraded synthetic labels while separately recording extraction accuracy and authorized human decisions. [R7]

ZERO’s 36 record pilot supports data pipeline development. It does not provide independent human labels or a representative population. The next step is blinded annotation by separate reviewers, disagreement analysis, and a governed held out set. Newly reviewed data should preserve the authored fixture’s identity rather than relabel it as observed evidence. [R8]

Waymax is an external autonomous driving research simulator, already referenced in GRI’s registry. Its bounding box behavior environment is distinct from raw sensor simulation and physical safety certification. Dataset access and license terms matter for any execution or redistribution. This review verified the reference, not an executed Waymax experiment or a deployed GRI integration. [R9, P4]

Evans Research remains a named lead. Scoped repository searches did not identify the exact source. It is recorded in the claim register so it remains part of the program without acquiring an invented identity or finding. Its resolution does not block the completed implementation package.

## Measurement of the AI Paradox

Use separate measures for separate mechanisms. Capability is observed task performance. Deployment is the scope and frequency of consequential use. Assurance is the evidence and institutional capacity available to evaluate that use. A time lag among these processes is a research measure, not automatic evidence of harm.

| Mechanism | Observable measure | Important alternative explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Authority expands before review | Time from material change to completed corresponding review | Low consequence changes may need less review |
| Provenance is mistaken for independence | Duplicate origin rate in claims asserted to be corroborated | Common reporting may be openly acknowledged |
| Metrics omit human outcomes | Divergence between task score and predefined human outcome | External conditions may explain the outcome |
| Oversight competence changes | Performance on independent diagnostic tasks over time | Hiring, turnover, or training may differ |
| Correction is inaccessible | Challenge completion, reversal, and remedy times | Some challenges lack sufficient evidence |

Missing review completion should be right censored at the analysis cutoff, not assigned a fictional completion date. Task success should be measured against a predefined primary outcome. Human outcomes should be defined with affected participants and analyzed separately. Avoid one composite paradox score until its measurement properties have been established.

Historical process tracing can identify sequences, contradictions, and plausible mechanisms. Prospective comparisons can test selected mechanisms. Neither alone licenses a universal causal claim across museums, mobility platforms, learning systems, and public agencies. The comparative contribution comes from explicit mechanisms and evidence that can challenge them.

A research lead owns the question and publication. A data steward owns access and retention. An evaluator owns a frozen evaluation set and reproducibility. A domain reviewer owns contextual assessment. A technical maintainer owns integration and rollback. One person can hold several roles, but conflicts and limits should be recorded.

Training a Tiger should initially inform a small simulator curriculum study, using forward versus backward sequence and matched disturbance exposure as distinct factors. The existing code package does not implement that simulator and does not claim training results. A Waymax study would be a separate mobility experiment. Neither should be treated as a prerequisite for completing evidence review and institutional research already possible with current assets.
