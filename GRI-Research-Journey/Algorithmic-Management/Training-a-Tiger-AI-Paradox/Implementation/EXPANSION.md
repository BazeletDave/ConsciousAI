## Corporate and jurisdiction comparisons

| Comparison family | Candidate subjects | Required distinctions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Road autonomy | Waymo, Zoox, Tesla Cybercab and other robotaxi programs | Announced capability, testing, authorized operation and measured outcomes; automation level, city, operating domain, miles or trips, incident definition and intervention access |
| Air mobility | eVTOL and air taxi programs | Aircraft certification, operator authority, airspace, pilot or supervision, infrastructure and commercial operation; electric or vertical flight does not establish autonomy |
| Industrial robotics | Humanoid and precision manipulation systems | Demonstration, pilot, production use, task range, worker interaction, exposure hours and failures; none is established by a product name |
| Institutional correction | Cambodian antiquities and the existing legitimacy case | Provenance challenge, review authority, source correction, remedy and changes in institutional practice |

Do not combine raw incident counts across firms without compatible exposure, observation windows and reporting rules. Do not infer safety from a publisher page changing. A reported product milestone is separate from an independently reviewed deployment outcome. The current six source observation targets are Waymo, Zoox, FAA, NHTSA, ANAC and Israel's Traffic Administration. Cybercab and humanoid robotics remain comparison watchlist subjects without a dedicated publisher connector in this edition.

Brazil, Israel and the United States are comparison jurisdictions, not interchangeable regulatory categories. Each comparison should identify agency, legal instrument, jurisdictional level, publication and effective dates, proposal versus enacted status, affected activity and operating conditions. Portuguese and Hebrew source interpretation may need qualified review. Geographic relevance is not evidence of a company's authorization to operate there.

## Cambodian antiquities as an institutional correction comparison

Use the existing [Cambodian antiquities and legitimacy case](../../../Integrated-Research-Journey/Legitimacy-Paradox-Cambodian-Antiquities.md) for an analytical comparison of provenance, challenge, institutional response and remedy. Map a challenged provenance claim to a challenged model or management claim; map review authority to accountable decision ownership; examine restitution and corrective action through their respective institutional processes. These are comparable governance questions, not equivalent harms, legal regimes or technical failure mechanisms.

The program defines correction register fields: challenged claim, prior and corrected source hashes, reviewer, decision, remedy evidence and review time. The register begins empty. No historical institution is assigned an automated misconduct label, and no correction is inferred solely from a missing or changed feed item.

## Ledger behavior and operational evidence

Each cycle records source snapshot age and explicit source failures, retains prior observations across outages, and appends first observation or metadata revision events. Event hashes form a chain; the snapshot also has a content hash. These detect accidental alteration, not an attacker who can rewrite all hashes. Git commits supply version history. The latest 500 events remain in the current payload; older events remain in prior commits with a retained chain anchor and archived count.

The initial local collection succeeded for Zoox, NHTSA, ANAC and Israel's agency page; Waymo and FAA returned transport errors in this environment. Failures are retained rather than reported as current observations. Existing country and mobility feed snapshots also contribute metadata, with their own observation times and source health. A page title or byte hash is a bounded observation, not an extracted deployment fact. Layout, tracking or access wall changes can alter page bytes.

Twelve new test methods cover repeated cycles, revision lineage, outages, staleness, malformed inputs, hash corruption, retained history, publisher failures, access walls and the HTTP endpoint. The broader local service suite ran 152 tests successfully after supplying the repository's existing image and engineering fixtures. The new endpoint returns 503 when its snapshot fails integrity checks. Deployment and scheduled execution must still be verified separately from local tests.
