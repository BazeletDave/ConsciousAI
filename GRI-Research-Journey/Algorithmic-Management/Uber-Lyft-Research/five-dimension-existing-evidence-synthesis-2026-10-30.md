# GRI Uber and Lyft Longitudinal Study | Five Dimension Evidence Synthesis

**Research status:** Retrospective, source linked analytical extension of the existing longitudinal algorithmic management program. 30 October 2026. No new participants recruited or data collected. No new research project established.

## Scope and evidence discipline

This synthesis applies five governance dimensions to already documented GRI evidence. A record is a **source specific claim or observation**, not a verified statement about Uber or Lyft internal algorithms. Source types remain separate: (1) GRI approved qualitative interview, (2) contributor supplied transaction screenshots transcribed into a case study, (3) driver narratives in an exploratory working paper, (4) corporate disclosure, and (5) publicly accessible discussion. Existing interview approvals are not equivalent to a prospective ethics determination for new recruitment or unrestricted reuse. All social media sources remain candidate material, not systematically sampled evidence.

## Five dimensional analytic codebook

| Code | Dimension | Operational coding question | Examples of evidence | Negative or alternative case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AUTH | Institutional authority | Who specifies the decision rule and what discretion does the driver retain? | Offer acceptance, cancellation, grant or restriction, appeals | Driver freely rejects offer without sanction |
| INFO | Information transparency | What price, time, fee, incentive, eligibility or decision rationale is available when action is required? | Offer card, fare breakdown, public platform documentation | Disclosed explanation accounts for variation |
| ECON | Economic consequence | What measurable cost, income, unpaid time or risk follows, with correct denominator? | Receipts, mileage, pickup time, costs, payout | Bonuses, service differences or accounting reconcile discrepancy |
| ADAPT | Human response and adaptation | How does the driver respond to incentives, uncertainty and platform decisions? | Selective acceptance, positioning, multi app use, appeals | Strategy has no measurable advantage after controls |
| ACC | Institutional accountability | Can a decision be explained, contested, independently audited and remedied? | Support records, correction, traceability, oversight | Transparent review resolves apparent anomaly |

For every coded record preserve source, original claim, observed versus reported status, geography and time window, platform, consent or public access basis, supporting documentation, uncertainty, contrary evidence and verification need. Do not infer intentional algorithmic behavior from an isolated outcome.

## Source triangulation plan already authorized for desk research

The [global mobility evidence protocol](2026-10-24-global-mobility-evidence-program.md) already identifies corporate pricing and transparency disclosures and illustrative Reddit threads. These are **leads**, not independently verified cross platform findings. Expand the existing source ledger by assigning claim type and dimension codes, preserving URL, retrieval date, source permissions and paraphrased content. Public YouTube and Facebook material can be evaluated where access and use are permitted, but do not scrape restricted groups or convert personal comments into a participant dataset without appropriate ethics and privacy review.

**Triangulation priority A:** Chicago transaction case: compare displayed estimated fee definitions with contemporaneous platform disclosures; request authorized final receipts and promotion breakdown before making compensation claims.

**Priority B:** Participant 0107: distinguish displayed trip duration from pickup, repositioning and online time; test cost assumptions with sensitivity bands and participant authorized records.

**Priority C:** Mobility accounts 001–003: build explicit competing explanations for boost or surge patterns, including demand, supply, refresh timing, eligibility and bonuses.

## Institutional interpretation

Use Roy C. Nelson's attributed institutional framework to analyze rules, actors, interests, power, economic incentives, information, legitimacy and capacity to contest decisions. Liberal market efficiency, structural distributional asymmetry and institutional capacity are competing interpretive lenses, not established causal mechanisms. GLOBE cultural dimensions remain reserved for separately justified intercultural analysis and are not inserted by default.

## Methodological gates

1. Conduct a second independent coder review and disagreement adjudication before reporting reliability or validated thematic prevalence.
2. Freeze a source level coding ledger and provenance audit; do not publish confidential interview originals or identifying details.
3. Keep direct interviews, platform disclosures, screenshots and public discussions analytically separate until corroborated.
4. Obtain ethics determination and consent where required for prospective driver interviews or systematic human participant analysis. No new recruitment or contact is claimed here.
5. Test negative cases and alternative explanations; publish no causal allegation without evidence.
