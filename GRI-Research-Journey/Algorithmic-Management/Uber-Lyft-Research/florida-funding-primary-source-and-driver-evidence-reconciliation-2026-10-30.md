# Primary Source Reconciliation | Florida Mobility Capital and Driver Unit Economics

**Existing GRI longitudinal algorithmic management study | 30 October 2026 | Desk review**

## Primary source findings

1. **Original statutory purpose.** [FDOT NEVI program](https://www.fdot.gov/planning/policy/ev/nevi-formula-program) describes approximately **$198 million** in five year formula funding intended for electric vehicle charging on designated highway corridors, including light duty vehicles. [FDOT funding overview](https://fdot.gov/emergingtechnology/home/evprogram/funding) specifies typical **80 percent federal / 20 percent applicant** funding shares. These are program descriptions, not a current disbursement ledger.
2. **Reported alternative use.** [Miami Herald reporting republished by WLRN, July 30, 2026](https://www.wlrn.org/light/environment/2026-07-30/florida-wants-to-use-cash-meant-for-electric-car-chargers-on-flying-cars-instead) describes FDOT's proposal to apply approximately **$197 million** to **32** aerial charging and landing sites, with a stated **$5.6 million per site** estimate. The reporting says federal approval of the alternative use remained unresolved at publication. **32 × $5.6 million = $179.2 million**; this is a conceptual arithmetic check, not evidence that the remaining difference is appropriated, committed, or spent. Site costs and allocation scopes may differ.
3. **Original Florida advanced air mobility plan.** [FDOT November 2025 Aerial Highway Network Business Plan](https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/aviation/aam/fdot-aam-business-plan-_nov_2025_final.pdf?sfvrsn=b9e6aa5c_1) documents planned regional phases including central Florida, South Florida, and Miami to Key West. This establishes state planning intent, not approval of NEVI repurposing or actual site construction.
4. **State program development.** [FDOT August 10, 2026 release](https://www.fdot.gov/info/co/news/2026/08102026) describes SunTrax testing and planned eVTOL work and references Waymo's Florida operations. This is a government announcement, not independent evidence of aviation certification or NEVI fund expenditure.
5. **Distinct federal NEVI repurposing.** [FHWA notice N4510.913, March 12, 2026](https://highways.dot.gov/laws-regulations/directives/notices/n-4510913) identifies a **nationwide $503,756,000** repurposing of unobligated NEVI funds under federal appropriations law. It must **not** be conflated with Florida's proposed $197 million air taxi use. Florida's state specific reduction, if any, requires the FHWA table and state ledger.

## What has not been established

The review did **not** retrieve an executed FHWA approval for Florida's specific alternative use, a state level NEVI obligation or expenditure ledger demonstrating $197 million spent on aerial sites, or final procurement awards. Therefore code Florida funding as **PROPOSED / REPORTED**, not **APPROVED**, **OBLIGATED**, **EXPENDED**, or **ON HOLD** as a formal financial status. The phrase 'on hold' requires a dated agency decision.

This is **not** evidence of a separate **$6.66 offer for 26 minutes**. The latter remains **USER SUPPLIED EXAMPLE / SOURCE NOT LOCATED**. Do not merge these records or claim the same trip.

### Reproducible unit economics

- **Participant 0107 (documented):** 33.3 miles / 20 MPG × $4.50 = **$7.4925 fuel**; $14.15 − $7.4925 = **$6.6575 after fuel only**, rounded **$6.66**. 46 minutes displayed travel: $6.6575 / (46/60) = **$8.68 per displayed trip hour after fuel only**. Pickup, waiting, deadhead, insurance, depreciation, maintenance and taxes are excluded. This is an illustration based on participant provided offer and assumptions, not verified actual net income.
- **Separate hypothetical example:** $6.66 / (26/60) = **$15.37 per displayed hour gross**, before all expenses; at 40 total minutes, **$9.99 per total hour gross**. The 40 minutes are hypothetical, not an observation.

**Illustrative sensitivity, not measured costs:** At a $14.15 offer and 33.3 trip miles, after fuel only residual varies with fuel price and MPG; the trip may also incur unpaid miles. Do not infer wage floor violations from the arithmetic alone.
