## Methodological reconciliation

Do not average hourly values whose denominators differ. **Engaged/active time** excludes some or all waiting and repositioning; **online time** includes availability; **shift time** can include off app work and unpaid repositioning. A dollar amount per trip cannot be converted to hourly net income without time, mileage and expense records.

Separate (1) gross receipts including tips and bonuses, (2) variable cash contribution after fuel and incremental costs, (3) fully allocated vehicle income after maintenance, insurance, depreciation and attributable finance, and (4) after tax personal income. Taxes and vehicle costs must not be double counted. A tax mileage benchmark is not a measured expense for every driver.

The existing three UberX screenshots and TPA waiting lot narrative are separate evidence records; they do not validate the national earnings claims. Market heterogeneity is a hypothesis requiring comparable samples and denominators.
