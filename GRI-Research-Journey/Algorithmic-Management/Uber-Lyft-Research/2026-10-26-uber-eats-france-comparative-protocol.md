**Grand Research Institute | ConsciousAI**

### Executive assessment
A viral October 25, 2026 claim alleges that couriers in France use multiple phones and reject low paying Uber Eats offers to induce higher subsequent offers. A contemporaneous October 26 account traces the claim to an Arabic language social post amplified by Breaking911 and explicitly reports that the alleged €15 outcome is **not independently verified**. Neither phone count nor sequential price observations alone demonstrates causal manipulation.

### Research questions
**RQ1:** Does declining a low value delivery offer predict a higher subsequent offer for the same or comparable task after controlling for market conditions?  
**RQ2:** How do couriers perceive, interpret, and adapt to platform allocation and payment systems?  
**RQ3:** How do statutory compensation guarantees, account integrity controls, and transparency rights shape adaptation and welfare?

**H1 (association):** More rejected offers in a narrowly defined market window correlate with higher subsequent offers conditional on observable demand, supply and trip characteristics.  
**H0:** Conditional association is zero.  
**H2 (alternative):** Apparent increases are explained by demand spikes, delays, time of day, task characteristics, selection effects, or platform policy rather than rejection itself.

### Measurement and causal identification
Unit: deidentified offer event. Fields: study pseudonym, platform, local timestamp, market cell, proposed gross pay, pickup/dropoff distance, estimated minutes, acceptance status, time until next offer, active/online/waiting time, observed weather, and source reliability. No names, immigration status, account credentials, biometric identifiers, or precise home locations.

Primary descriptive estimand: difference in mean log offer compensation following high vs low observed market rejection periods, adjusted for task and market covariates. This is **not** a causal estimate without a credible identification strategy.

Preferred design: voluntary, consent based prospective panel of lawful account holders across matched French markets; repeated observations and worker interviews; city by hour fixed effects and task controls; robustness checks for composition and demand shifts. A causal design requires an exogenous shock or defensible natural experiment; do not assert identification from simple before/after sequences. Do not organize strategic rejection campaigns, use unauthorized accounts, or induce platform rule violations.

Outcomes: compensation per offer, gross earnings per active hour, net earnings per total work hour, waiting time, rejection rate, distance, and dispersion across couriers. Explicitly distinguish **online**, **active**, and **total work** hours. France's September 2026 weekly active time guarantee is a key regulatory covariate and a potential discontinuity, not an automatic causal instrument.

### Quality controls
- Preregister operational definitions, sampling criteria, and exclusion rules before primary data collection.
- Use provenance, hashes, collection timestamps, permission basis, and evidence classification in the GRI evidence ledger.
- Check missingness, duplicate observations, survivorship, confounding, temporal leakage, and changes in platform terms.
- Report effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, robustness specifications, and null findings.
- Independent reviewer should verify any claim elevated from allegation to finding.

### Institutional governance lens
**Access → Capability → Authority → Consequences → Feedback** maps platform participation, allocation systems, platform decision rights, worker welfare, and adaptation loops. Compare French evidence with separate Uber/Lyft United States research only after documenting regulatory and market differences. Do not treat taxi/ride hail findings as directly applicable to food delivery.

### Research ethics and dissemination
The viral description's use of 'migrant' is an unverified attribution and must not be treated as a mechanism or proxy. Protect participants, avoid stigmatization, comply with applicable data protection law, and obtain necessary ethics review before human subjects recruitment. Public materials should not reproduce private class images or courier identifiers without appropriate permission.

**Publication decision:** Retain as a validated *research question* supported by contextual official evidence and adjacent scholarship; keep the alleged repricing outcome classified **UNVERIFIED**.
