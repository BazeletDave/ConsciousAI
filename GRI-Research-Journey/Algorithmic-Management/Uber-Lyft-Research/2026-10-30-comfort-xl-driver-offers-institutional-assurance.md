## Dated external benchmarks
AAA Florida gas price page on October 30, 2026 reports Miami metro regular $3.9948 per gallon, Florida $3.9154, Naples $3.9782. https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=FL . These are contemporary illustrative prices, not fuel prices necessarily prevailing when screenshots were captured.

IRS business standard mileage rate July through December 2026 is $0.76 per mile, versus $0.725 January through June. https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates . It is a standardized tax benchmark including fixed and variable vehicle costs, not verified actual expenses; do not add gasoline, depreciation, insurance or financing to it.

## Transparent scenario comparison
Assume regular gasoline $3.9948/gallon and 25 MPG, excluding return travel and idle time.

C01 fuel = 121.9 / 25 * 3.9948 = $19.48; residual after fuel only $69.32. IRS proxy = 121.9 * $0.76 = $92.64; offer less proxy = -$3.84.

X01 fuel = 60.8 / 25 * 3.9948 = $9.72; residual after fuel only $53.42. IRS proxy = 60.8 * $0.76 = $46.21; offer less proxy = $16.93.

Illustrative full empty return of C01 passenger distance adds 119.5 miles, giving 241.4 total miles and about $38.57 fuel at 25 MPG, leaving about $50.23 after fuel before return time and other costs. This is a scenario, not a measured return trip. Actual Comfort and XL vehicle efficiency, fuel grade, insurance and capital costs may differ substantially.

## Receipt reconciliation and vehicle cost verification

The offer screenshots do not establish completed trips or final earnings. To calculate realized income, obtain authorized receipts, actual pickup and passenger miles, waiting and repositioning time, return travel, vehicle model, actual fuel consumption, insurance, maintenance, depreciation, financing and tax assumptions. Keep actual observations separate from hypothetical scenarios.

For C01, a hypothetical 119.5 mile empty return raises combined distance to 241.4 miles. For X01, a hypothetical 55.3 mile empty return raises combined distance to 116.1 miles. Neither return is documented. At 25 MPG and an illustrative $3.9948 per gallon, the resulting fuel estimates are $38.57 and $18.55, respectively. These are not net income calculations, and no return time is assumed.

For independent verification, retain an authorized original evidence reference, anonymized record ID, source date if known, transcription check, calculation version, and reviewer signoff.
