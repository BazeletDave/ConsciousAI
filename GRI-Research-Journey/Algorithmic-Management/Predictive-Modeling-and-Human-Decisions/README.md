# Predictive Modeling and Human Decisions | GRI Research Journey

**Research lead:** David Grand, Grand Research Institute  
**Status:** Exploratory, prospective research protocol | October 11, 2026

[**Read the working research protocol: From Prediction to Algorithmic Management**](./From-Prediction-to-Algorithmic-Management-2026-10-11.md)

This project examines whether AI forecasting workflows learn from verified feedback and improve *measured* decision quality, and how human acceptance or override of recommendations changes management accountability. Sports outcomes provide a transparent testing domain, but the emphasis is model validation, uncertainty, algorithmic management, and governance rather than wagering.

**Planned figures:** evidence completeness; reliability and calibration; paired model score comparisons; human decision behavior; longitudinal learning versus a frozen baseline; management governance process.

**Evidence limitation:** The initial discussion contains four candidate forecast statements rather than a verified outcomes dataset. No measured improvement, accuracy rate, or autonomous foundation model learning is claimed. The companion October 11 PDF framework exists separately and is not included in this repository folder.
