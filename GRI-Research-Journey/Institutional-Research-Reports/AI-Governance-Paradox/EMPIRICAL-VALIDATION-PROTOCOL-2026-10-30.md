# Empirical Validation Protocol: Custody, Intertemporal Justice and Resilience
**David A. Grand | Grand Research Institute | 30 October 2026**
**Classification:** Research design and evidence register; not independently validated results. Extends the existing AI Governance Paradox without revising original findings.

## Objective and testable claims
Assess whether cryptographically valid transactions can coexist with unauthorized institutional outcomes, whether nominal repayment differs from economic restoration, and whether public financing and software markets allocate technological benefits and resilience equitably.

### Claim and evidence taxonomy
Use **confirmed primary record**, **attributed reporting**, **analytical inference**, **hypothesis**, and **unknown**. For every claim retain exact URL, publication date, access date, original wording or short extract, calculation method, confidence, and correction history. Reproduce no personal or sensitive operational information.

## Case comparison protocol
**Unit of analysis:** one incident or procurement/program decision at a stated date. Record actor, jurisdiction, authority chain, public/private financial exposure, asset custody model, verified loss, reported impact, frozen assets, recovered assets, contractual obligations, policy intervention, time to restoration, and stakeholder distributions. Maintain **FTX fraud and bankruptcy**, **Binance alleged operational compromise**, and **NASA procurement** as distinct mechanisms.

**FTX counterfactual:** Actual Anthropic sale proceeds were reported near $1.3B in 2024. An illustrative $72B scenario requires 8% ownership and $900B hypothetical equity value. This is not FTX's current holding, independently verified value, or customer entitlement. Calculate at multiple dated valuations and ownership/dilution assumptions; label every scenario. Compare ex-ante sale decision to later hindsight, considering fiduciary duties, liquidity needs, time discount and risk.

**Binance incident:** Build a chronology from independently dated source records. Separate impact, unauthorized outflow, frozen tokens, recovered tokens, reserve replacement and paid customer compensation. Test EIP-7702 only if raw chain data, code delegation targets and independently verified wallet ownership are available. Do not infer AI causation or intentional inside participation.

**NASA procurement:** Analyze contract awards, performance milestones, cost and schedule against alternative contracting mechanisms. Distinguish government contract revenue from subsidy and from personal enrichment; assess whether public investment delivered documented public capability and introduced supplier dependency.

**Software access:** Predefine product class and functional comparability; measure subscription cost as % of disposable income, device/connectivity requirements, supported languages, accessibility and service continuity. Control for purchasing-power parity, education, regional availability and differences in capability.

## Reproducible instruments
### Dataset schema
`case_id, observation_date_utc, actor, mechanism, claim_id, evidence_status, source_url, source_date, access_date, original_unit, original_value, normalized_value, normalization_method, uncertainty_note, reviewer, revision_id`.

### Outcomes
* Authorization: false-approved transactions / attempted unauthorized transactions; median detection and revocation latency.
* Custody: realized loss / customer liabilities; frozen versus recovered fraction; days until audited reserve restoration.
* Intertemporal justice: nominal and inflation-adjusted creditor recovery; crypto-unit equivalent; opportunity cost sensitivity.
* Sustainability: cost-to-income access ratio; service uptime and recovery; public expenditure per delivered unit; concentration of suppliers; environmental resource intensity where data exist.
* Evidence integrity: primary-source coverage, unresolved contradiction count, independent reproduction rate.

### Design safeguards
Use pre-registered hypotheses P1-P13 from the companion research, synthetic fixtures for authorization tests, independent reviewer signoff, uncertainty ranges, sensitivity tables and negative-result publication. Descriptive event-study associations are not proof of causation. Avoid treating a single case as representative of all blockchain or public procurement systems.
