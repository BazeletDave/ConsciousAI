# The AI Governance Paradox

David A. Grand | Grand Research Institute  
Institutional Research Report | October 2026  
Presentation revision: 28 October 2026 | 22 pages

[Read the institutional research report (PDF)](GRI_AI_Governance_Paradox_Institutional_Research_Report.pdf) · [Readable text companion](REPORT.md)

This report examines algorithmic management, global leadership, institutional learning, and accountability as AI assumes greater responsibility for consequential decisions. It contributes to GRI's mission to advance responsible artificial intelligence through transparent governance, empirical research, and accountable technological innovation.

## Scope and reading guide

| Pages | Research focus |
| --- | --- |

| 5 to 8 | Workforce evidence, governance standards and global management scholarship |

| 21 to 22 | Evidence status, continuing research and references |

Published external evidence, GRI analytical propositions, and completed software checks have distinct evidentiary roles. The report proposes institutional measures and research hypotheses; it does not claim that the framework has demonstrated causal effectiveness.
