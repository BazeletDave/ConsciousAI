## Research collection priorities
- Verify original academic event notes and ASU public-source provenance.
- Document driver offer economics including declined offers, waiting, deadhead mileage, tolls and opportunity costs.
- Record driver-selected academic schedules and sleep constraints only with consent.
- Determine whether human-subjects or university ethics review is required before recruiting working students.
- Compare rules-based scenarios against subsequent ML only after adequate data and safeguards exist.
- Conduct a bibliographic and evidence audit before publication.

## Methodological boundaries
Faculty and university contributions are credited as educational context, not endorsements. Public statements are evidence of stated institutional goals, not proof of program outcomes. No nonpublic platform API access or dispatch capability is assumed. The driver remains the final decision-maker. Publish no identifying driver, student or passenger information.
