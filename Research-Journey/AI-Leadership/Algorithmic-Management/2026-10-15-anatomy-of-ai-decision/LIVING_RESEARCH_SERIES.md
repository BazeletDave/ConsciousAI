Grand Research Institute | Foundational edition | 15 October 2026

## October 2026 foundational research publication
A 17-page PDF, **The AI Paradox: A Living Research Journey in Artificial Intelligence, Leadership, Data, and Algorithmic Management**, has been prepared in the GRI research session. The PDF is a separate research artifact and is not yet stored as a binary asset in this repository.

### Research proposition
As AI improves prediction, the organizational bottlenecks shift toward data readiness, judgment, objectives, accountable action and institutional learning. The unit of analysis is the decision system rather than the model alone.
