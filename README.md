# Grand Research Institute
### ConsciousAI | Global Governance Intelligence

**Independent research led by David Grand**

Grand Research Institute (GRI) studies how artificial intelligence, algorithmic management, and digital infrastructure reshape organizational authority, institutional capacity, economic development, and human accountability. **ConsciousAI** is a living research and engineering ecosystem: new evidence is evaluated and integrated into established research programs rather than presented as a succession of announcements or standalone initiatives.

GRI draws on interdisciplinary scholarship, field research, technical experimentation, and graduate-level management study, including academic learning at Thunderbird School of Global Management, Arizona State University. GRI operates independently; references to academic institutions, governments, scholars, or other organizations do not imply their sponsorship, review, or endorsement.

## Research method and evidence standards

GRI distinguishes primary sources, peer-reviewed scholarship, reported claims, longitudinal field observations, research hypotheses, synthetic experiments, institutional attestations, and independently reproduced results. Research conclusions are bounded by their documented methods, provenance, access permissions, and limitations. New observations are integrated into the existing ecosystem through versioned records and explicit cross-references.

Publicly released materials may be examined under their applicable licenses. Restricted research evidence, confidential reviews, participant information, and proprietary intellectual property remain subject to authorized access and appropriate safeguards.

[Confidential review and responsible disclosure policy](GRI-Research-Journey/Research-Governance/ConsciousAI-Research-Systems/CONFIDENTIAL-REVIEW-RECONCILIATION.md)

## Research engagement

Researchers and institutional reviewers may propose corrections, cite source records, or request appropriately scoped private verification through authorized GRI contact channels. Public GitHub issues should not contain confidential or security-sensitive material. Requests for restricted evidence are evaluated individually and do not guarantee access or certification.
