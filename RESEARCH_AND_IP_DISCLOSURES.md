# Research Integrity, Institutional Independence, and Intellectual Property

Grand Research Institute LLC (GRI) is an independent research organization. References to Thunderbird School of Global Management, Arizona State University, U.S. agencies, companies, research institutes, or their personnel are educational or analytical references, **not endorsements, partnerships, government authorization, or certification**, unless documented independently.

## Evidence classifications
Clearly distinguish: primary verified evidence; attributed public reporting; interview or participant observations collected with authority; synthetic demonstrations; historical exercise materials; hypothesis and commentary; and fictional creative work. Do not promote a simulated result or a declared verification flag into an independently measured outcome. Human review, contestability, correction, and provenance must remain visible.

## Regulated or sensitive claims
Statements concerning medical effects, impairment, research trials, safety, performance, legal compliance, elections, or government systems require specific dated supporting sources, limitations, and appropriate expert review. Historical exercise stakeholder quotations are not automatically verified interviews. Do not imply clinical, IRB, FDA, Treasury, CISA, or university approval without evidence.

## Licensing and third-party rights
A repository-level MIT software license may grant broad rights to software and included materials within its scope. **Do not assume it preserves exclusivity of novels, cover art, video, audio, datasets, trademarks, or licensed media.** Before publishing or re-licensing, inventory rights, provenance, permissions, fair-use analysis where relevant, and third-party licenses. This document does not retroactively modify existing licenses or withdraw previously granted rights.

## Jurisdiction and legal review
U.S. federal and Florida requirements, plus foreign law where applicable, must be assessed against actual operations, data subjects, services and transactions. No blanket global compliance claim is made. Florida entity standing, contracts, tax, consumer protection, privacy, cybersecurity, research ethics, copyright and sector-specific obligations require separate verification.
