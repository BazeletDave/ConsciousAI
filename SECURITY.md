# Security Policy — Grand Research Institute LLC

This public repository is an independent research and prototype archive, not a production security certification or an authorized government service.

## Report a vulnerability
Do **not** publish exploit details, private records, credentials, or personal information in public issues. Contact the repository owner privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature when enabled, or an established verified GRI contact channel. Do not assume a public issue is confidential. Do not conduct intrusive testing against GRI services or third-party systems without explicit authorization.

## Supported scope
Public prototypes are research-only unless a particular deployment explicitly documents production support. A merged pull request or passing test is not proof of operational safety, regulatory compliance, or an independent audit.

## Handling and response
GRI will triage reported issues according to severity, preserve evidence appropriately, restrict access, and assess applicable notification obligations with counsel. No guaranteed response SLA is represented here. Avoid committing secrets, tokens, participant information, private interviews, regulated records, or unlicensed third-party material. Rotate any exposed credential immediately and investigate its history.

## Deployment gate
No sensitive-data intake or public evidence submission until identity, authorization, data minimization, retention, encryption, incident response, backups, and independently tested restoration are approved and documented.
