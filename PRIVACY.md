# Privacy and Research Data Handling — Preliminary Policy

**Grand Research Institute LLC** publishes independent research, software prototypes and educational material. This document describes repository practices; it is **not** a substitute for a complete website/app privacy notice or legal determination of statutory applicability.

## Public repository boundary
Do not publish research-participant identities, raw interview recordings, precise locations, protected health data, credentials, private correspondence, client information, or identifiable ride histories. Use consent, legal authority, de-identification and documented human review before any release. Do not assume hashed identifiers are anonymous.

## Collection and processing
The public GitHub archive is not an authorized personal-data intake channel. Any future collection must document purpose, lawful basis where required, notice, data minimization, access controls, retention/deletion schedule, third-party processors, cross-border transfers, and incident procedures. Do not claim that public-source material is free of privacy obligations.

## Rights and incident response
Direct privacy concerns privately to the repository owner via an established verified GRI contact channel; do not post personal details in public issues. Applicable access, correction, deletion, opt-out and breach notification rights depend on jurisdiction and processing context. Requests require identity verification and a documented handling process.

## Important distinction
GitHub, Render, Vercel and other third-party services have their own policies and data processing terms. Their practices are not fully described by this repository policy. A deployment-specific notice is required before collecting visitor or participant personal information.

**Status:** Governance baseline; legal and operational review pending. No claim of comprehensive U.S., Florida, EU, UK, or worldwide compliance.
