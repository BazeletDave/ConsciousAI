THE NOVEL

What happens when every system is working exactly as designed?

The Back Door explores a world in which artificial intelligence, surveillance, autonomous technology and institutional authority no longer operate as separate systems. The danger is not necessarily a machine breaking its rules. It is what can happen when interconnected systems follow their rules perfectly while the humans responsible for them no longer understand the whole.

“Every system was following its rules. That was the problem.”

THE WORLD OF THE BACK DOOR

People. Places. Data. Power. Consequences.

Autonomy

Systems increasingly interpret information, recommend actions and act before institutions can respond.

Governance

Authority becomes harder to locate when responsibility is distributed across algorithms and organizations.

Surveillance

The same technologies that create safety and convenience can create unprecedented visibility and control.

Human Judgment

The decisive question is not whether technology can act. It is who retains the authority to decide when it should.
