01 / The premise

When systems converge, who is accountable?

The Back Door imagines a world where algorithms, connected infrastructure and institutional decisions are no longer separate. Each system may be functioning as designed. Together, they create consequences no single operator intended—or can fully explain.

Set against the Florida Keys, the story places human judgment at the center of a technological landscape moving faster than its institutions.

The danger isn't always a system that breaks its rules. Sometimes it's a world in which every system obeys them.

A fictional narrative exploring real questions about autonomy, accountability and authority. Story details and characters are fictional.

03 / The world

A familiar coastline. An unfamiliar system of power.

The setting brings together the intimacy of island communities and the scale of global technological infrastructure.

Florida Keys

Water, bridges and isolated corridors create a distinctive landscape where physical and digital systems intersect.

Connected Infrastructure

Transportation, mobility, communications and surveillance become parts of a shared technological environment.

Global Consequences

Local decisions connect to broader questions of trade, institutional legitimacy, governance and control.
